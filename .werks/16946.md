# Label filter: long labels no longer overflow

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-11-17T08:57:33+00:00
version    | 2.5.0p22
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | multisite
level      | 1
compatible | yes



Long host or service labels in the label filter of views and dashboards
pushed the operator dropdowns and the remove buttons out of the filter
panel. Since the panel cannot scroll horizontally, such entries could
not be removed anymore without resetting the whole filter.

Long labels are now truncated with an ellipsis, the full label is shown
on hover.
