# OpenTelemetry RED latency: latency in seconds shown 1000x too low

component  | telemetry
level      | 1
compatible | no

The _Latency (HTTP)_ service showed latencies 1000 times too low when your
instrumentation reports `http.server.request.duration` in seconds, the unit
the stable OpenTelemetry HTTP semantic conventions define. A request that took
200 ms showed up as 0.20 ms, so upper levels from the _OpenTelemetry RED
Latency_ ruleset never triggered.

The check assumed every latency histogram was in milliseconds. It now reads
the unit of each series and converts seconds (`s`) and milliseconds (`ms`)
correctly. Services that receive milliseconds, like _Latency (RPC)_ and usually
_Latency (span-based)_, show the same values as before.

If one of your _Latency_ services receives seconds, its graphs jump by a factor
of 1000 at the update, and levels you configured may now trigger for the first
time. That's the correct value showing up, not a new problem.

## Incompatible: series with another unit or no unit

If a series reports any other unit, or none at all, the service now goes
`UNKNOWN` for that time window and names the unit. Before, it showed a value
that might be off by a factor of 1000. To fix this, set the histogram's unit to
`s` or `ms` in your instrumentation.
