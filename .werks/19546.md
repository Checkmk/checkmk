# Relay: Renew the certificate early and retry until it succeeds

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-10-27T16:07:22.701174+00:00
version    | 2.5.0p20
class      | fix
edition    | ultimate
component  | relay
level      | 2
compatible | yes

Previously, the relay requested a new client certificate only during the
last day before the current one expired, and it tried once or twice. If
the site was not reachable at that time, the relay could be left without
a valid certificate.

The relay now starts to renew its certificate after two thirds of the
certificate's lifetime. With the usual validity of three months, this is
around day 60. The relay tries immediately, and then again after a random
time of 6 to 24 hours, until the renewal succeeds. The random gap prevents
a problem that occurs at the same time every day from blocking all
attempts.

The relay log now tells you clearly what happened:

* A successful renewal is logged as "Certificate rotation successful."
* A failed renewal names the step that failed, the error, and how long
  the current certificate is still valid, for example:
  `Certificate rotation failed while requesting a new certificate from the site: <error>. 29 days of certificate validity remaining`.
  If the certificate has already expired, the log says how long ago.

No action is required.
