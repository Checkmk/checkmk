# Welcome page: shorter onboarding, grouped host options

The onboarding on the welcome page now has three steps: "Add your first host", "Enable notifications" and "Create a dashboard". Activating changes is now part of "Add your first host", and the separate folder step was removed. "Assign responsibilities with contact groups" and "Adjust service thresholds" appear as optional guides once the onboarding is complete. Progress only counts the three onboarding steps, so the counter may change after the update.

"Add your first host" now groups its options by system type and links to the Proxmox VE and Kubernetes Quick Setups. In editions with Checkmk Relay, it explains when a relay is needed and links to its setup. In Checkmk Cloud, network devices and Proxmox VE stay greyed out until a relay is configured. Once one exists, they can be added through it, and a new SNMP host is preset to use a configured relay.
