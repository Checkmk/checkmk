Monitor > Overview now has a _Network devices_ dashboard, right below
_Linux hosts_ and _Windows hosts_. It shows the health of your switches,
Fibre Channel switches, routers, firewalls, wireless controllers and
appliances.

## What it shows

* Device states and service statistics
* Counts of `CRIT` data sources, interfaces, power supplies and fans, and
  temperatures
* Top 10 lists for interface bandwidth and errors, temperature, lowest
  uptime and slowest SNMP polling
* A device table with inventory data

## Which hosts are included

Hosts whose label `cmk/device_type` is `switch`, `router`, `firewall`,
`wlc`, `fcswitch` or `appliance`. Checkmk sets this label from the SNMP
system description. To include other devices, clone the dashboard and
adjust its filters.
