# dell_poweredge_status: tolerate empty SNMP data

On iDRAC/PowerEdge hosts the "PowerEdge Health" service went to UNKNOWN with
<tt>IndexError: list index out of range</tt> and produced a crash report when
the SNMP walk under the Dell iDRAC health tree returned no rows.

An empty response from the device is now treated as "no section data": the
"PowerEdge Health" service reports that no data was received instead of
crashing.
