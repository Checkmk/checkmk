# apc_inrow: support newer InRow cooling units such as the ACRC301S

Newer APC InRow cooling units such as the ACRC301S no longer provide the
airIRRC objects of the PowerNet-MIB that the services _Temperature_, _Airflow_
and _Fanspeed_ read. On these units Checkmk discovered none of those services,
although the SNMP walk clearly contained the readings.

These units publish their readings in the generic cooling unit status tables
of the PowerNet-MIB instead. The plug-ins `apc_inrow_temp`, `apc_inrow_airflow`
and `apc_inrow_fanspeed` now read these tables as well, so the services are
discovered on newer units too:

* _Temperature_: one service per sensor the unit reports in degrees Celsius,
  e.g. _Temperature Supply Air_, _Temperature Rack Inlet 1_ or
  _Temperature Entering Chilled Water_.
* _Airflow_: the unit's airflow in liters per second.
* _Fanspeed_: the fan speed in percent.

Older InRow units that provide the airIRRC objects are monitored as before.
Note that the default lower levels of _Airflow_ (500/200 l/s) stem from older
units. A smaller unit running at low fan speed may report less and go `WARN`;
adjust the ruleset _Airflow levels_ in that case.
