# apc_symmetra: Apply configured replacement state to battery pack cartridges

---------- | ---

version    | 2.5.0p16
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

Werk #18343 added the states of the individual battery pack cartridges to
the "APC Symmetra status" service. A cartridge flagging "Needs Replacement"
was always reported as WARN, regardless of the option "State if battery
needs replacement" in the rule "APC Symmetra Checks". The option only
affected the UPS-wide battery replace indicator, which some devices do not
raise even though one of their cartridges reports the flag.

The configured state is now applied to the "Needs Replacement" flag of the
cartridges as well. With the default (WARN) the service behaves as before.
All other cartridge flags, such as overvoltage or overtemperature, are
still reported as WARN.
