# Relay: Next check time no longer in the past after a forced check

version    | 2.5.0p22

component  | relay
level      | 1
compatible | yes

Previously, after you used _Reschedule active checks_ on a service that a
relay runs (an active check, _Check_MK_ or _Check_MK Discovery_ of a relay
host), the service details showed the _Time of the next scheduled service
check_ in the past, e.g. "97 s in the past!". The time kept growing and
even survived a core restart.

The relay, not the Checkmk Micro Core, schedules these checks. The core
now clears the next check time once such a forced check has run. It also
clears times left over from before this fix when it loads its state. The
service details now show "Managed by the relay" in this field.
