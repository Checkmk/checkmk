# NetApp ONTAP SVM traffic: configurable latency levels

This werk impacts those who monitor NetApp ONTAP systems.

The *Traffic SVM* services (`netapp_ontap_vs_traffic`) reported the latency of
the CIFS, FCP and iSCSI protocols without ever leaving the state OK.

The new rule *NetApp SVM traffic* configures upper levels on the read and
on the write latency. The levels are applied to the latency of every protocol
the service reports.

No levels are configured by default, so the services keep their current state
until a rule is created.

Thanks to Luca Barelli for contributing the fix.
