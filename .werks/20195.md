# infoblox: Fix crash on unknown NIOS service IDs

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-08-18T13:38:38.188009+00:00
version    | 2.3.0p50
class      | fix
edition    | cre
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

The _Service ..._ and _Node service ..._ services of an Infoblox appliance could
disappear completely. The discovery of the affected host reported

_Parsing of section infoblox_node_services failed - please submit a crash report!_

and the crash report showed

```
KeyError: '60'
```

NIOS 9.1 reports three service IDs that Checkmk does not know yet. A single
unknown ID in an active state made the whole section fail to parse, so no
Infoblox service was discovered on that appliance at all.

Unknown service IDs are now ignored. All services Checkmk does know are
discovered and monitored as before. The services that are new in NIOS 9.1 stay
unmonitored until their names are published and can be added.
