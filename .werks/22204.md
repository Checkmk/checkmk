# checkpoint_vpn_tunnels: don't crash on unknown link priority

---------- | ---
date       | 2026-09-15T09:16:23.536239+00:00
version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

If a Check Point appliance reported a VPN tunnel link priority other than
Primary, Backup or On-demand, service discovery failed with
`KeyError: '4294967295'` and the `Check_MK` service went to WARN.

This was observed on tunnels spanning multiple interfaces: they report a link
priority of 4294967295 instead of one of the three documented values.

Such tunnels are now inventorized with their raw link priority shown as
`Unknown (<value>)`, and discovery completes as before.
