We have redesigned _Setup > General > Global settings_ and the site-specific
settings of a site in _Setup > Distributed monitoring_. It makes it easier to
find a setting, to see what it is set to, and to understand where that value
comes from.

## Finding a setting

The search field above the topics filters the settings by their title or
internal name as you type, without reloading the page. Next to the search, the
chips _All settings_, _Modified only_ and _Site overrides only_ narrow the list
down to the settings you have changed or that sites override. In
_Global settings_, _Site overrides only_ appears as soon as at least one site
overrides a setting.

The search, the chosen chip and the setting you are editing are kept in the
URL, so you can bookmark a view or send a colleague a link to a setting.

## Editing a setting

Clicking a setting opens it in a slide-in on the same page, so you keep your
place in the list.

When you edit a setting in _Global settings_, the slide-in lists every site
that overrides it, each with a link to the site-specific settings of that site.
You see right away whether your change affects all sites or whether some of
them keep their own value.

## Site-specific settings

The site-specific settings of a site use the same page and tell the two sources
of a value apart. _Modified only_ shows every setting configured for this site,
whether in _Global settings_ or as an override, while _Site overrides only_
shows just the settings this site overrides.

When you edit a setting there, the slide-in also shows the value from
_Global settings_ that the site uses as long as it has no override. Removing
the site-specific value makes the site fall back to exactly that value.

Links and bookmarks to the previous pages, including links to a single setting,
keep working for one major release. Please update them before upgrading to
3.1.0.
