# No superfluous vertical scrollbar on anchored dashboards

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-09-03T12:47:46.602905+00:00
version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | multisite
level      | 1

On an anchored dashboard that is too narrow for its widgets, Chrome showed a
vertical scrollbar next to the horizontal one, even though there was nothing to
scroll to. Firefox was not affected, and neither were responsive dashboards.
