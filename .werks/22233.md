# Service name filters now sit at the top of the filter list

---------- | ---

version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | feature
edition    | community
component  | multisite
level      | 1
compatible | yes

The filters "Service name (regex)", "Service name (exact match)", and
"Service alternative display name (regex)" are no longer hidden inside a
collapsed "Service name" group. They are now listed at the top of the
filter list, so you can reach them without expanding a group first. The
"Service name" group has been removed.

This applies to the dashboard filter panel and the dashboard wizard, and
to the service filters of a Checkmk RRD query in the custom graph
designer.

The set of available filters and their behavior are unchanged. Search
across all filters works as before.
