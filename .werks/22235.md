# Widget titles missing after switching dashboards

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-09-17T12:47:53.524071+00:00
version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | multisite
level      | 1
compatible | yes

When you picked another dashboard from the dashboard selection at the top of the
page, the titles of all widgets could be missing. The widgets themselves were
shown and worked normally, only their titles were gone. Reloading the page
brought them back.

Dashboards with many widgets were affected more often. The titles are now
computed once per dashboard, so they no longer go missing.
