# Renamed the "Release flags" section in Global settings to "Experimental flags"

The **Release flags** section in Global settings has been renamed to **Experimental flags** to match Checkmk's terminology for these temporary, time-limited feature toggles.

This is a naming change only: the flags themselves, their behavior, and their removal deadlines are unaffected.
Existing flag state is not carried over automatically.
