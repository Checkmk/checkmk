# Apply individual Checkmk service states to special agents

The rule "Status of the Checkmk services" offers "Individual states per data source" with an entry "Special agent".
This entry was never applied: special agents always used the "Overall status" settings instead.

With this werk, the "Special agent" entry is applied to all special agents of a host.
If you have configured it, the state of the "Check_MK" service may change after the update.
