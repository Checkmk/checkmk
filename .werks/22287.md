# apc_symmetra: Report exceeded battery lifetime of battery pack cartridges

Some APC UPS devices report that a battery needs to be replaced only through
the health of their battery pack cartridges ("Battery lifetime exceeded,
replace battery"). They raise neither the UPS-wide battery replace indicator
nor the "Needs Replacement" flag of the cartridge (see Werk #20115). On these
devices, the "APC Symmetra status" service stayed OK although the battery had
to be replaced.

The service now also evaluates the health of the battery pack cartridges. An
exceeded battery lifetime is reported with the state configured in the option
"State if battery needs replacement" of the rule "APC Symmetra Checks"
(default: WARN). Other health information, such as a battery lifetime near
its end or a lifetime warning that was acknowledged on the device, is shown
in the service summary but does not change the state of the service.
