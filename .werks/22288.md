# Passive Checkmk services no longer change state outside their check period

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-10-23T10:06:57.021291+00:00
version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | pro
component  | cmc
level      | 2
compatible | yes

Services with a rule "Check period for passive Checkmk services" were sometimes
checked outside their time period. They could then change state, for example
from OK to CRIT, and send notifications.

Before it checks such a service, the Check_MK service asks Livestatus whether
the time period is active. If Livestatus did not answer within two seconds, for
example because all its connections were busy, the service was checked as if
the time period were active.

The Checkmk Micro Core now discards results of such services that arrive
outside their time period.
