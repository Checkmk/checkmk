## Widgets no longer reload after the dashboard opens

After a dashboard opened, widgets such as Host statistics, Service
statistics, Host state and Service state reloaded two to three times
before they settled. In distributed setups with slow remote sites, this
took several seconds. The widgets now load once. After that they only
update on their regular refresh or when you change their filters.

## Errors no longer hidden behind the loading icon

When a widget could not load its data and you changed its filters, it
kept showing the loading icon if the next attempt failed as well. It now
shows the error.

[Werk #22235](https://checkmk.com/werk/22235) removed a duplicate
request for the widget titles. Together, the two werks restore the
loading behaviour of Checkmk 2.4.
