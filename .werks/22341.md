# Reports: prevent a graph's heading from landing on a different page than its graph

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-09-03T09:14:15+00:00
version    | 2.4.0p37
class      | fix
edition    | cee
component  | reporting
level      | 1
compatible | yes

A graph report element's heading was not guaranteed to land on the
same page as its graph: whenever the heading fit in the space
remaining on a page but the graph didn't, the heading ended up alone
at the bottom of that page while the graph started on the next one.

Before adding the heading, the report now works out the graph's
exact rendered height and breaks to a new page early if needed, so
the heading and its graph always land together.
