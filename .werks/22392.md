The rule *NetApp Snapvaults / Snapmirror Lag Time* is now called
*NetApp SnapVault / SnapMirror*. Existing rules are kept. The item of the
rule is now called "Destination" instead of "Source path", because the item
has always been the destination path of the relationship.

Previously, the *Snapvault* services showed the SnapMirror transfer state, but
this result was always OK (see werk #17937). A failed transfer therefore did not
raise an alert.

The service now evaluates the transfer state. By default:

* "failed", "aborted" and "hard_aborted" are CRIT
* "queued", "success" and "transferring" are OK
* any other transfer state reported by ONTAP is UNKNOWN

The service also shows whether ONTAP reports the relationship as unhealthy,
together with the reasons from ONTAP. An unhealthy relationship is CRIT by
default.

You can change these states with the rule *NetApp SnapVault / SnapMirror*.

## Compatibility

This werk is incompatible because the update changes your monitoring without
any change to your configuration:

* *Snapvault* services can go CRIT after the update. This happens if the
  relationship reports a failed or aborted transfer, or if ONTAP reports the
  relationship as unhealthy. If you do not want these alerts, set the
  matching states to OK in the rule *NetApp SnapVault / SnapMirror*.
* Relationships without a lag time now also get a service, for example a
  relationship whose first transfer failed. Run a service discovery on the
  affected NetApp hosts to add these services.
