On the classic "All hosts" view, the licensing banner of the Checkmk Cloud
(SaaS) edition covered the top right of the page, hiding the button that opens
the new "All hosts" view underneath it.

The button now sits in the shortcut area of the page menu bar in the SaaS
edition. The other editions keep it where it was.
