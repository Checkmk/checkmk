# SaaS: view switcher and feedback link are no longer hidden

---------- | ---

On the new host and service pages, the licensing banner of the Checkmk
Cloud (SaaS) edition covered the top right of the page. The button that
switches back to the classic view, the button that opens the new view
from a classic one, and the feedback link were hidden underneath it.

All three now move into the shortcut area of the page menu bar in the
SaaS edition. The other editions keep them where they were.
