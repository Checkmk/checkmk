# dashboards: host and service level filters taken into account by widgets

The "Host service level" and "Service service level" filters applied the
selected range only in views, not in dashboard widgets such as "Host
statistics" or "Host overview". Widgets showed numbers for all hosts or
services with any service level.

The range is now evaluated by the monitoring core, so widgets, views and
reports filter consistently. Only service levels configured under
"Global settings > Event Console > Service levels" are matched.
