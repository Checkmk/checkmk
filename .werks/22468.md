# Veeam CDP jobs: Show result of the last session and age of the last restore point

The _VEEAM CDP Job_ services reported _Time since last CDP Run_ based on the
wrong value. The Windows agent plug-in sends the policy's next scheduled run
(`NextRun`), but the check treated it as the time of the last run. A CDP policy
replicates continuously, so `NextRun` only contains the time of the query: the
age shown was meaningless, and the service could go `WARN` with "The timestamp
of the file is in the future".

In addition, a failing CDP policy was shown as `OK`: on Veeam Backup &
Replication 13 the policy state is only `InitialSync`, `Running` or `Disabled`,
so the check never saw a failure. `InitialSync` was reported as `UNKN`.

## What changed

The services now show:

* the policy state, with `InitialSync` being `OK`
* the result of the last finished session of the policy (`Warning` is `WARN`,
  `Failed` is `CRIT`). Veeam closes a CDP session about once a day and records
  in its result, for example, an exceeded RPO, failed long-term restore points
  or a powered-off source VM.
* the time since the last long-term restore point. For a policy with several
  replicas, the oldest of their latest long-term restore points is used.

The time of the next run is no longer shown.

The ruleset _Veeam: Time since last CDP run_ is renamed to _Veeam: Time since
last CDP long-term restore point_. Your existing rules are kept. Disabled
policies stay `OK`: they are not checked for their age, and the result of their
last session is only shown in the details.

The time since the last long-term restore point does not reflect the RPO: CDP
replicates continuously, while long-term restore points are created on their
own schedule, by default every 8 hours. Long-term restore points that Veeam
created incomplete are counted as well. Review the default levels of 30/48
hours against the schedule of your policies.

## Action required

Redeploy the _Veeam_ agent plug-in (`veeam_backup_status.ps1`) on your Veeam
servers, e.g. by baking and updating the agents. Until then, the services only
show the policy state, and your levels are not applied.

Note that the updated agent plug-in requires a Checkmk server that includes this
werk. Older servers will lose the _VEEAM CDP Job_ services.
