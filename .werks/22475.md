# ntp: report why a falseticking peer is critical

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-09-22T10:48:11.424966+00:00
version    | 2.5.0p15
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

If the NTP client rejected one of its peers as a falseticker, the corresponding
`NTP Peer` service went UNKNOWN with
`ValueError: 'summary' must be non-empty str or None` and produced a crash report
instead of a result. The check yielded the CRIT result for that peer without any
text.

The service now reports `State: falsetick` as the reason for the CRIT, matching
how the other peer states are shown.
