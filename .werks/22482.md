# Prediction graph: no red critical area for time slots without levels

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-09-24T09:00:00.000000+00:00
version    | 2.5.0p15
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | multisite
level      | 1
compatible | yes

The prediction graph of a service with predictive levels could be flooded
with the red critical area. This happened for every time slot for which no
levels could be computed, e.g. because levels based on the standard deviation
need at least two samples of history for a slot. This is typically the case
in the first weeks after setting up predictive levels with the period "Day of
the week". Such slots are now left blank.

In addition, the vertical axis of the graph now always includes zero values
and ends slightly above the highest critical level, so the critical area stays
visible instead of being cut off at the top.
