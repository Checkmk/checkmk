# acme_voltage: Fix crash during service discovery

---------- | ---

On ACME Packet (Oracle) session border controllers, the service discovery
crashed with `ValueError: too many values to unpack (expected 3)` as soon as
the device reported voltage sensors, so no `Voltage <sensor>` services could
be discovered.

Discovery now works again: every present voltage sensor gets its own service,
sensors reported as not present are skipped as before.
