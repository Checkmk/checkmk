# dashboards: Share with contact groups and sites without Setup permissions

class      | fix

Previously, users without the *User management* permission could not
share a dashboard with contact groups. On the "Access" tab of the
dashboard settings, "Members of contact groups" was greyed out, even
with *Publish dashboards to allowed contact groups* or *Publish
dashboards to foreign contact groups*. Users with *User management* were
offered all contact groups, also those they were not allowed to share
with, and saving then failed with an error. In the same way, "Users of
site" offered no sites to users without the *Site management*
permission.

This has been fixed. As described in [Werk #19989](https://checkmk.com/werk/19989), the dialog
now offers the user's own contact groups with *Publish dashboards to
allowed contact groups*, and all contact groups with *Publish dashboards
to foreign contact groups*. With *Publish dashboards to users of
selected sites*, it offers the sites the user is authorized for. *User
management* and *Site management* are no longer needed.
