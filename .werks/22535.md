# AWS/EBS Limits: Count io1/io2 IOPS

class      | fix

compatible | yes

The service _AWS/EBS Limits_ checks the IO operations per second (IOPS)
of Provisioned IOPS SSD volumes (io1 and io2) against the AWS limits.
Previously, it reported the storage of these volumes in GiB instead of
their IOPS. The reported usage was therefore far too low and hardly
ever reached your warning or critical levels.

The service now reports the sum of the provisioned IOPS of all io1 and
io2 volumes. After the update, the reported values are much higher. The
service may change to `WARN` or `CRIT` if your volumes come close to
the limit.
