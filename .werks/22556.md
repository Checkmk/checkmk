# Personal notification rules only cover own hosts and services

Personal notification rules were not limited to the hosts and services their
owner is responsible for. A user who may edit their personal notification
settings could receive notifications, including the check output and labels,
about hosts and services they are not a contact of. A site condition of the
rule could also include sites the user is not authorized for.

Personal notification rules now only cover the hosts and services their
owner is responsible for:

* A personal rule only applies to hosts and services its owner is a contact
  of. If the contacts of a host or service cannot be determined, personal
  rules do not apply. Personal rules that cancel notifications are not
  affected.
* A personal rule only applies on sites its owner is authorized for, even if
  the rule lists other sites. If none of the listed sites is left, the rule
  no longer applies.

This also applies to users with the permission "See all host and services":
Their personal rules only cover hosts and services they are a configured
contact of.

Existing rules are not changed. Rules that reach beyond the owner's own hosts,
services or sites no longer apply there. The notification analysis under
*Setup > Events > Notifications* shows why such a rule did not match.

If users need notifications about hosts and services they are not a contact
of, create a global notification rule instead.

### Who's Affected

All editions are affected. A site is affected if users without
administrative permissions hold the permission "Edit personal notification
settings", which the built-in role "Normal monitoring user" has by default.

### Affected Versions

* 2.5.0
* 2.4.0
* 2.3.0 (EOL)
* 2.2.0 (EOL)

### Mitigations

If updating is not possible, remove the permission "Edit personal notification
settings" from all roles whose users should not receive notifications about
hosts and services they are not a contact of. Review the existing personal
notification rules of your users (see next section).

### Indicators of Compromise

Check the personal notification rules of your users (*Setup > Users > Users*,
action "Notification rules" of each user) for rules that match hosts or
services outside the user's responsibility, for example rules without
conditions.
