# Product usage analytics: first transmission within 24 hours

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-11-03T16:25:35.270712+00:00
version    | 2.5.0p19
class      | feature
edition    | community
component  | wato
level      | 1
compatible | yes

When you enable product usage analytics for the first time, your site
now transmits its first usage data within 24 hours. Previously, the first
transmission came at a random time between 1 and 30 days later. After
that, the site transmits its data every 30 days, as before.

Sites that already have a planned transmission keep it. This includes
sites where you switch product usage analytics off and on again. The
content of the data does not change.
