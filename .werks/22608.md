# Kubernetes: no crash on future conditions

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-10-06T05:23:51.798868+00:00
version    | 2.4.0p38
class      | fix
edition    | cre
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

If the clock of a Kubernetes cluster ran ahead of the clock of the
Checkmk server, the `Condition` service of a pod could crash with
`ValueError: Cannot render negative timespan` while a condition was
in an unexpected state, because the condition appeared to have
changed in the future.

The affected condition now goes to UNKNOWN, states how far its last
transition lies in the future and asks you to check the system time.
The other conditions are reported as before.
