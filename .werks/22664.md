# esx_vsphere_licenses: Apply configured levels again

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-10-08T14:53:45.148999+00:00
version    | 2.5.0p16
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

The _License_ services of VMware ESX and vCenter hosts ignored their levels
and stayed `OK` no matter how many licenses were in use. This affected the
default setting _Go critical if all licenses are used_ as well as levels
configured in the ruleset _VMware licenses_ (_Absolute levels for unused
licenses_ and _Percentual levels for unused licenses_). This regression was
introduced in Checkmk 2.5.0.

The configured levels are evaluated again. After the update, services whose
licenses are used up, or whose unused licenses fall below your configured
levels, change to `WARN` or `CRIT`.
