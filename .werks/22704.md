# NTNX Alerts: fix the "Prism Central only" option

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-11-03T06:29:23.155662+00:00
version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

The option "Prism Central only" of the rule _Nutanix Prism Alerts_ never matched
any alert. With the option set, the _NTNX Alerts_ service always showed `OK`
with "No alerts".

The option now keeps the alerts about the Prism Central VM. These are the
alerts that Nutanix marks with the VM type "Prism Central VM", such as
"Prism Central VM rebooted", and the alerts whose affected entity is the
Prism Central VM, such as its disk space alerts. If you use this option, the
service may change to `WARN` or `CRIT` after the update.
