# Update did not convert site-specific global settings on central sites

---------- | ---

version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | wato
level      | 1
compatible | yes

On central and standalone sites, the update did not process the local
site's site-specific global settings (`etc/check_mk/*/wato/sitespecific.mk`).
Remote sites were updated, but on all others these settings were left as they
were. Renamed settings were not migrated, and settings that no longer exist,
or that the edition you updated to does not offer, stayed in effect. For
example, after an update to Checkmk Cloud, real-time checks could stay enabled
for the local site, even though Checkmk Cloud does not offer them.

The update now processes the site-specific global settings of every site.
