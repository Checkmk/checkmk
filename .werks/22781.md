# Meraki licenses: per-device licensing

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-10-19T07:10:29.828633+00:00
version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

For Cisco Meraki organizations that use the per-device licensing
model, the Meraki API reports no overall license status, expiration
date or licensed device counts. The `Licenses` services of all
organizations of the monitored Meraki account went UNKNOWN, and the
crash report showed `ValidationError: 2 validation errors for
LicensesOverview`.

Organizations with co-termination licensing are monitored as before.
The `Licenses` service of an organization with per-device licensing
now goes UNKNOWN and states that per-device licensing is not
supported. Such organizations are not added to the HW/SW inventory.
