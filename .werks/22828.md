# Cisco Meraki: check sensor readings of zero

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-11-24T15:55:35.832763+00:00
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

The Cisco Meraki sensor services _Cisco Meraki Temperature_, _Cisco Meraki
Battery Percentage_ and _Cisco Meraki Humidity Relative Percentage_ ignored
readings with a value of exactly 0. In that case the service showed no value,
no metric and no alert.

The worst case was an empty sensor battery: with lower levels configured in
the _Battery levels_ ruleset, the battery service went `CRIT` at 5 %, but not
at 0 %. Likewise, a temperature of 0 °C was not checked against your
configured levels.

A reading of 0 is now checked like any other value. Sensors that report no
reading at all are still not checked against your levels, as before.
