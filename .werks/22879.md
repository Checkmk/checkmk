# EC: service levels sort by number

Views sorted by the service levels of hosts or services, e.g. by the columns
"Service service level" or "Host service level", compared the levels as text.
With the service levels 5, 10 and 100 they ended up in the order 10, 100, 5.
They are sorted by their numbers now. Hosts and services without a service
level still come first.
