# Kubernetes CronJob: no crash on future times

key        | value
---------- | ---
date       | 2026-11-02T13:06:47.192381+00:00
version    | 3.0.0b1
class      | fix
edition    | community
component  | checks
level      | 1
compatible | yes

If the Kubernetes cluster reported the start time of a pending or
running job, the last successful completion or the last schedule of a
CronJob a few seconds after the current time of the Checkmk server,
the CronJob `Status` service crashed with
`ValueError: Cannot render negative timespan`.

The service now reports such a timestamp as being in the future and
asks you to check the system time, like the other Kubernetes checks.
