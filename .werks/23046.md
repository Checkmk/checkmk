# Trusted CAs: no crash on negative serial numbers

Recording a change of the trusted certificate authorities crashed with
"Certificate with a negative serial number" when the stored list
contained such a certificate. This happened when editing the global
setting "Trusted certificate authorities for SSL" (for example to
remove such a certificate), when importing a site CA on the
"Distributed monitoring" page, and when rotating the site CA. The new
setting itself was saved, but the pending change was not created.

Certificates with a negative serial number are now included in the
audit log like all other trusted certificate authorities, and the
change is recorded.
