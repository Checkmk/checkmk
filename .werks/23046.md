# Trusted CAs: no crash on negative serial numbers

---------- | ---

class      | fix

Saving the global setting "Trusted certificate authorities for SSL"
failed with "Certificate with a negative serial number" when the list
contained such a certificate.

Certificates with a negative serial number are now skipped with a
warning when the change is recorded, as they already were everywhere
else, and the setting is saved.
