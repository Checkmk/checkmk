# The Decision as a Short Statement

## Motivation

Why the decision is needed: the problem it solves and the constraints it has
to meet.

## Decision

What is decided, in enough detail to build it, and why this way.

## Consequences

- What changes for users and developers.
- Limitation: what the decision does not solve.
- Follow-up: work it causes.

## Rejected Ideas

### A Rejected Idea

What it would have been and why it lost.
